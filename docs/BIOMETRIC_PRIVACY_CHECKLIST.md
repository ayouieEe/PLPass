# Biometric Privacy Checklist

Facial descriptors are biometric personal data even when the original photo is not stored. Complete this checklist with the institution's privacy officer or authorized adviser before production.

- Document the lawful purpose, scope, and accountable data controller.
- Present clear consent and an equivalent non-biometric attendance alternative.
- Record when and how consent was obtained and withdrawn.
- Limit descriptor lookup to an owned ongoing session and registered participant.
- Audit every descriptor creation, access, replacement, and deletion without logging the descriptor.
- Define retention periods for photos, descriptors, requests, audit logs, and backups.
- Provide processes for access, correction, withdrawal, deletion, and complaints.
- Encrypt transport and storage; restrict administrative and backup access.
- Prohibit descriptors, photos, service keys, and sensitive metadata from logs and analytics.
- Document accuracy limitations, false-match handling, manual review, and non-discrimination safeguards.
- Test breach response, credential rotation, session revocation, notification, and evidence preservation.
- Review applicable institutional rules and Philippine privacy requirements with qualified personnel.
