# Organizer and Student UAT Checklist

Record tester, role, device, browser, date, environment, result, evidence, and issue reference for every scenario.

## Organizer acceptance

- Sign in, recover from invalid credentials, and sign out.
- View accurate dashboard counts and analytics.
- Create an event; verify required fields, participants, schedule conflicts, and objectives.
- Start and end an owned attendance session.
- Record attendance through QR, facial backup, and manual fallback.
- Confirm duplicate, invalid, outside-window, and unauthorized attendance attempts are rejected.
- Review, approve, and reject correction/credential requests with required reasons.
- Export event attendance and summary reports.
- Verify notifications, audit logs, profile update, responsive navigation, keyboard access, and readable contrast.
- Confirm another organizer's records are inaccessible.

## Student acceptance

- Sign in, recover from invalid credentials, and sign out.
- View only the signed-in student's events, attendance, requests, notifications, and profile.
- Open pending feedback and late-reason tasks.
- Submit a valid late reason and event feedback in the required order.
- Submit attendance corrections and issue reports; verify validation and history updates.
- Use QR readiness and facial enrollment/re-enrollment flows.
- Deny camera permission and confirm the fallback remains understandable and usable.
- Verify responsive navigation, keyboard access, zoom/reflow, and readable contrast.
- Confirm organizer routes and another student's records are inaccessible.

## Acceptance decision

- No unresolved critical/high security, privacy, data-loss, or role-isolation issue.
- No blocker in required organizer or student workflows.
- Known medium/low issues have an owner, target date, workaround, and documented capstone limitation.
- Organizer representative, student representative, adviser/product owner, and development lead sign off.
